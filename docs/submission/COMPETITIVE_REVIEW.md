# Competitive review and judge strategy

This is a quality-bar review, not a claim that every named project is entered in the same 2026 category or remains unchanged.

## What strong hackathon products do well

Projects such as AgentSentry and SpecLoom communicate one legible risk, make the AI system observable, and provide a complete product story rather than a feature collection. The broader quality set recorded in the master brief—AgentSentry, Chaperone, Firsthand, Buyable, PlateGap, SpecLoom, Rapport, and MisconceptionMap—also reinforces four patterns:

1. Real deployment evidence beats architectural promises.
2. A bounded workflow is easier to understand and trust.
3. Transparent limitations improve credibility.
4. A visible before/after story is more memorable than a dashboard tour.

## ParcelProof's competitive answer

| Judge dimension | ParcelProof evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Technical innovation | Bedrock is constrained to observation; validated deterministic policy owns dispatch authority. |
| Implementation quality | Live AWS vertical slice, private uploads, strict schemas, correction history, CDK, automated tests, and safe failure paths. |
| Market impact | A concrete last-mile control point for small personalised sellers where barcode-only checks are incomplete. |
| Creativity and storytelling | The Ayesha/Alisha mismatch creates an immediate problem, visible block, correction, and verified recovery. |

## Positioning to preserve

- Do not claim ParcelProof invented packing verification.
- Do not claim the synthetic set is customer data or a measured accuracy benchmark.
- Lead with the wrong-to-corrected parcel story, not the service list.
- Demonstrate `BLOCK → correction → PASS` and mention `REVIEW` as a deliberate safety outcome.
- Keep the commercial next step credible: a seller pilot measuring false passes, false blocks, review rate, latency, and correction loops.

## Biggest remaining presentation risk

The product is technically submission-ready, but a weak or missing video would leave its strongest advantage—the correction loop—buried. The 60–90 second recording should therefore be treated as the final high-priority owner task.
